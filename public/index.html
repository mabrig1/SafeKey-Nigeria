BEFORE YOU PAY

Your property safety checklist.

Save these questions before your next site visit or negotiation.

  1. Who legally owns the property, and what official record supports that claim?
  2. Can a licensed professional confirm the seller's authority and documents?
  3. Do the site's physical location and survey boundaries match the advertisement?
  4. Who will receive the payment, and how is that recipient authorized?
  5. What independent evidence protects you if the deal fails?
