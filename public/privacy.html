PILOT DATA NOTICE

Privacy & limitations

Updated 11 November 2026. This page describes the MVP's current code design. Public operation requires a named data controller, valid contact point, lawful-basis review, grievance procedure and compliance assessment before collecting real users' data.

What is processed

The preliminary check processes property details, contact information and advertisement language supplied by you to produce an explanation. In the stateless preview, screening inputs are not saved. When a database is configured, it stores protected-hash identifiers for property/agent/listing correlation, a case ID, risk level and time. Raw phone numbers, full listing URLs and property addresses are not stored in check history. The hashing secret is configured privately on the server. No official agency database or online property listing is queried.

Two-listing comparison

The optional cross-listing comparison uses information entered into the two forms. Links are parsed as text and never fetched from the advertised sites. SafeKey does not store the two listings or their results. If a D1 database is configured, a protected rate-limit counter may be retained briefly to prevent abuse; its presence does not prove any property connection.

Local evidence kit

Evidence files are fingerprinted in your own browser with SHA-256, without uploading their contents to SafeKey. The selected files and their fingerprints remain only in this browser session unless you explicitly download an evidence-manifest JSON file. Downloaded manifests contain file names, timestamps, fingerprint values and any notes you typed. Store them privately. File fingerprints cannot establish the authenticity, ownership or legal validity of the document.

Private due-diligence workspace

Your checklist, updates and notes remain in this browser tab only. The feature does not send them to SafeKey or third parties, nor does it automatically save them in local storage. You can deliberately download a JSON copy and load it back; downloaded files may contain confidential notes. The external land-administration links open agency websites and are not integrated title searches. Checklist statuses are self-reported and cannot certify ownership or authorize payment.

Private reports

If reporting is enabled, an incident report stores its free-text description, general area, category, evidence type, review status and timestamps. Avoid submitting bank numbers, identity documents or sensitive third-party information. Reports are available only through the private admin API; they are not published automatically. A report is an allegation, not a confirmed scam.

Optional third-party services

When you tick the optional AI checkbox and this service is enabled, only the property category and generic warning-code identifiers are transmitted to OpenRouter for three suggested questions. Personal names, addresses, phone numbers, listing text and report allegations are not sent. Where email notifications are enabled, Resend receives the random private report reference and the designated moderator email address, not the allegation text or reporter's details. These services are disabled by default. Data-controller arrangements and third-party processing require review before a public launch.

Private professional case desk

When separately enabled, staff may record private case UUIDs, transaction type, general jurisdiction, an optional professional-directory reference, and structured review activity. The case desk stores no buyer name, property address, contact information, document contents, allegation text or free-text review notes. It never publishes results or certifies title, identity, documents or payment safety. Case records are intended to expire after 90 days only where the operator has configured and validated scheduled database cleanup; backups and other retention obligations require separate management.

Retention

Automated check records are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Pending and closed-unsubstantiated incident reports are scheduled for deletion after 180 days. Substantiated or referred reports are retained until manual review under a documented lawful retention policy. These are design defaults, not a guarantee that all backups are deleted at the same instant. Rate-limit identifiers are hashed and retained up to roughly 48 hours.

Your rights and safety

Do not treat any risk-screening result as proof of ownership, authentication, legal advice or a guarantee of safe payment. The pilot does not verify credentials, receive documents, process money or provide escrow. Before public launch, operators must provide an actual privacy contact and user rights workflow for access, correction, objection and deletion requests.

Security

Transport security, private D1 access, secret-managed hashing and an administrator API key are provided by the architecture. Production launch requires additional controls: staff identity management, Cloudflare Access, WAF/anti-abuse measures, legal review and an incident-response procedure.
